FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

We're Here to Help

Find quick answers to common questions about our products, applications, and best practices.

Coverage varies by product and application. Generally, G3 covers approximately 200-300 sq ft per gallon on pathways. For specific calculations, use our online coverage calculator or contact our support team with your project dimensions.
Initial drying occurs within 24-48 hours depending on weather conditions. Full cure time is typically 7-14 days. We recommend keeping traffic off treated areas during the initial drying period for best results.
Yes! All TechniSoil products are eco-friendly and non-toxic once cured. They're safe for pets, plants, and the environment. Our formulations are water-based and contain no harmful chemicals.
Absolutely! Most TechniSoil products are designed to be DIY-friendly. Application is straightforward with basic tools like a pump sprayer or watering can. We provide detailed instructions and video tutorials to guide you through the process.
G3 is our standard pathway stabilizer, perfect for residential applications and moderate traffic. G5 is our premium formula with enhanced durability, longer lifespan, and better performance in challenging conditions. Choose G5 for high-traffic areas or when maximum longevity is required.
Yes! TechniSoil products are excellent for slopes and hillside applications. They help prevent erosion and keep materials in place. For steep slopes or areas prone to heavy water flow, we recommend G5 or consulting with our technical team for best results.
