Company Growth

OUR JOURNEY

2011

In a humble metal building with no restroom, TechniSoil sprouted, fueled by a single employee's passion. Pathway Stabilizer® emerged.

2012

The NanoPave® launch stirred the market, garnering warm reception. Another team member joined the fold.

2014

Innovation blossomed as EkoFlo® came into being and took flight. This major triumph propelled ongoing growth.

2015

TechniSoil found a new abode, complete with a restroom. BarkBinder® soon followed, making its grand debut.

2017

The innovative Molecule™ series of sealers took shape—meticulously developed, rigorously tested, and introduced to market.
