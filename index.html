Landscape

G3 Pathway® Stabilizer

A liquid polymer stabilizer for natural pavement applications like residential pathways and planter beds. Designed to bind decomposed granite (DG) and crushed stone, reducing dust and erosion while maintaining a natural look.

Available Sizes

1 Gallon, 5 Gallons, 55 Gallons

Landscape

SandPave Pro®

A high-strength, easy-to-use binder for concrete sand. It creates a strong, pliable, and abrasion-resistant surface for pathways and patios without requiring compaction. Works best with 1/8" Concrete Sand.

Available Sizes

1 Gallon

Landscape

G3 Commercial Surface®

An extra-strength liquid stabilizer designed for heavier foot traffic areas such as commercial walkways, patios, and common areas. Works with DG and crushed stone to create a durable, natural surface.

Available Sizes

1 Gallon, 5 Gallons, 55 Gallons, 275 Gallons

Landscape

EkoFlo® Permeable Pebble Binder

A binder that mixes with clean, dry pebbles to create stunning, permeable walkways, patios, and tree wells. It is UV-stable and allows water to flow through, aiding in storm-water management.

Available Sizes

16 oz, 1 Gallon, 5 Gallons

Xeriscape

Ground Cover Stabilizer™

Designed to preserve the natural surface texture of crushed stone mixes and ground cover. It helps reduce maintenance and water usage, making it ideal for xeriscaping.

Available Sizes

1 Gallon, 5 Gallons

Landscape

BarkBinder®

Often described as "hairspray for mulch," this liquid stabilizer locks shredded bark and mulch in place, limiting wind and water erosion while being safe for plants and animals.

Available Sizes

1 Gallon, 5 Gallons, 55 Gallons, 275 Gallons

Landscape

DustCube™

A ready-to-use liquid polymer emulsion for seasonal dust control. Designed for non-vehicular and light usage areas such as dirt lots, construction sites, and fields.

Available Sizes

250 Gallon Totes

Hardscape

NanoPave® JSS

A 2-in-1 product that stabilizes jointing materials (sand/crushed stone) and seals the surface. Suitable for joints up to 4 inches wide on pavers, flagstone, and natural stone.

Available Sizes

1 Gallon, 5 Gallons, 55 Gallons, 275 Gallons

Hardscape

NanoPave® Hardscape Sealer

A water-based, VOC-free nano-polymer sealant that enhances and protects the natural beauty of hardscapes. Available in 'Natural Look' and 'Enhanced-Look' finishes.

Available Sizes

1 Gallon, 5 Gallons, 55 Gallons, 275 Gallons

Masonry

Molecule™ SRS

A penetrating sealer that creates a waterproof, breathable layer of stain resistance. Protects pavers, natural stone, concrete, and brick from oil, water, and chemical stains.

Available Sizes

5 Gallons

Masonry

Molecule™ [HS]

A "wash and wax" style product that cleans hardscapes and adds a layer of SRS protection. It helps restore the color and vibrancy of old, weather-worn paving.

Available Sizes

1 Gallon, 5 Gallons, 55 Gallons, 275 Gallons
