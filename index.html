Company Growth

Our Journey

As Technisoil journeyed through the years, it morphed and adapted, staying true to its roots in the landscape industry while remaining a vanguard of sustainable design and innovation.

2011

In a humble metal building with no restroom, Technisoil sprouted, fueled by a single employee's passion. Pathway Stabilizer® emerged, and we set sail in California.

2012

The NanoPave® launch stirred the market, garnering a warm reception. Another team member joined the fold.

2014

Innovation blossomed as Ekoflo® came into being and took flight. This major triumph propelled the company's ongoing growth.

2015

Technisoil found a new abode, complete with a restroom. Barkbinder® soon followed, making its grand debut.

2017

The innovative Molecule™ series of sealers took shape as they were meticulously developed, rigorously tested, and ultimately introduced to the market with great anticipation.
