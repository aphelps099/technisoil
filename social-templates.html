Brand Guidelines

Colors

Use navy (#1a3a52) for professionalism, sand/brown for natural earth tones, and green (#00845f) for sustainability messaging.

Typography

Inter font (300-700 weight). Keep headlines bold and large, body text clean and readable. Avoid excessive bold weights.

Imagery

Use high-quality photos of actual installations, product bottles, or natural landscapes. Avoid stock photos when possible.
